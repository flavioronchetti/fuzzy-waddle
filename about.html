Our Story

A family legacy rooted in Como since 1998.

From Family Table to Lake Como Destination

Ristorante Lago d’Oro was founded in 1998 by the Conti family, who dreamed of sharing the soul of Como through food. What began as a small trattoria near the water evolved into an elegant gourmet destination beloved by locals and travelers.

Today, the second generation carries the same values: warm service, uncompromising quality and a deep respect for seasonal ingredients. Every evening, we honor our heritage through refined interpretations of Lombardy classics and lake fish specialties.
